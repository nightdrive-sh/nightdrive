# AirPlay Receiver

Mirror your iPhone, iPad or Mac to this computer, like macOS's AirPlay Receiver: its screen opens in a window here, and music and videos sent with AirPlay play here too. It uses UxPlay, an open-source AirPlay server.

- On the iPhone or iPad: Control Centre › Screen Mirroring › this computer.
- The device asks for a PIN the first time: it shows in the middle of this screen. A new one comes with each login and after sleep.
- The window opens along the right edge. Move it anywhere, and close it with ⌘W.
- Viewing only: the mouse doesn't control the phone.
- Up to 4K, and smooth even when the picture moves: the installer adds FFmpeg's HEVC decoder from RPM Fusion.

Homepage: https://github.com/FDH2/UxPlay
Needs: an iPhone, iPad or Mac on the same network
